📖 Learn About Jesus

From miracles to Easter to Pentecost — let's walk with Jesus!

15

Jesus Does Wonderful Things ✨

▼

Jesus did wonderful things that were beyond human power. We call these miracles.

🍞🐟

Feeding 5000

From a boy's 5 loaves and 2 fishes, Jesus fed 5000 people — and 12 baskets were left over!

⛵🌊

Calming the Storm

A strong wind and waves came on the sea. Jesus spoke — and the storm became quiet!

👧✨

Raising the Little Girl

Jesus brought a little girl back to life — showing power even over death.

🛌➡️🚶

Healing the Paralyzed Man

"Your sins are forgiven. Get up and walk!" — and the man stood up and walked.

"Jesus took the loaves, gave thanks, and distributed them to those who were seated; so also the fish, as much as they wanted." — John 6:11
16

We Believe in Jesus 🙏

▼

Jesus is God's Son and the Saviour of the World. People came to him with great faith.

👁️‍🗨️ The Blind Man

He cried out: "Jesus, help me!"
And Jesus opened his eyes.

⚔️ The Centurion

His servant was very sick. The Centurion said: "I am not worthy that You should enter my house." Jesus was so happy with his faith that the servant was healed at once!

💡 Did you know?

At every Mass, before Holy Communion, we still say the Centurion's words: "Lord, I am not worthy that you should enter under my roof, but only say the word and my soul shall be healed."

17

The Best Gift of All — Holy Communion 🍞🍷

▼

The Last Supper was the special meal Jesus shared with his Apostles. Jesus took bread and wine and changed them into his own Body and Blood.

🍞 Bread + 🍷 Wine → ✨ Body & Blood of Jesus

This is called the Holy Eucharist — Jesus' best gift to us!

⛪ Our Bishop

Bishop Aldo Berardi is the Bishop of Bahrain.

🧎 Our Parish Priest

Rev. Fr. Francis Joseph, at Sacred Heart Catholic Church, Bahrain.

Only a priest can change bread and wine into the Body and Blood of Jesus at Mass.

18

Jesus Dies for Us ✝️

▼
🍞
HOLY THURSDAY (Maundy Thursday)

The Last Supper

Jesus shared bread and wine with his Apostles. Then he prayed in the Garden of Gethsemane. "Maundy" means Command.

✝️
GOOD FRIDAY

Jesus is Crucified

Jesus carried his cross and fell three times. Simon of Cyrene helped him. He was crucified at Mount Calvary. Mother Mary and Saint John stood by the Cross until the end.

⚰️
HOLY SATURDAY

Jesus is Buried

Joseph of Arimathea and Nicodemus placed Jesus' body in the tomb.

💛 Why is it called "GOOD" Friday?

Because Jesus died for us, and by his death he opened the gates of Heaven. That is the very best news of all — so the day is called Good Friday.

19

Jesus is Raised to a New Life — Easter! 🌅

▼

🌅 Easter Sunday

On the third day, Jesus rose from the dead! He showed his great power over death.

👩 First to See Him

Jesus first appeared to Mary Magdalene at the empty tomb.

😢 Judas — the one who betrayed

Sadly, one of Jesus' Apostles, Judas Iscariot, betrayed Jesus for thirty pieces of silver.

20

Jesus Begins the Church ⛪

▼

The Church is not just a building — it is a community of people who believe in Jesus and are baptised.

👴

St. Peter

Our first Pope!

👑

Pope Leo XIV

Our current Pope — the leader of the Catholic Church on earth and a successor of St. Peter.

⛪

Our Parish

Sacred Heart Catholic Church, Bahrain

👼 Pentecost — when the Church was born!

Mary and the Apostles were praying together. Suddenly a strong wind came, and tongues of fire rested on each Apostle. They were filled with the Holy Spirit and could speak many languages! "Pentecost" means 50th day.

21

Jesus Goes Back to Heaven — Ascension ☁️

▼

For 40 days after Easter, Jesus stayed with his disciples. Then he ascended to Heaven from the Mount of Olives. Eleven Apostles watched him go.

"Someday, Jesus will come again." — Angels to the disciples

Before going, Jesus promised to send the Holy Spirit to help his disciples. And he promised: "I will come again at the end of the world."

22

The Holy Spirit Comes — Pentecost 🔥

▼

On the 10th day after the Ascension (the 50th day after Easter), the Holy Spirit came in tongues of fire on Mother Mary and the Apostles.

🔥 What happened next?

  • The Apostles became brave!
  • Peter began to preach the Good News to crowds.
  • People believed and were baptised — in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
23

The Blessed Trinity 🔺

▼

There is only ONE God — but in THREE Persons. We call this the Blessed Trinity. It is a holy mystery.

👑
God the Father
created us
⬇️ One God ⬇️
✝️
God the Son · Jesus
saved us
⬇️ One God ⬇️
🕊️
God the Holy Spirit
makes us holy

✝️ The Sign of the Cross

When you make the sign of the cross, you say: "In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit." — That is a sign of the Blessed Trinity!

God's most precious gift to us is his own Son, Jesus. Jesus gives himself to us in the Holy Eucharist at every Mass.

27

Following Jesus — Living Like Him 🚶✨

▼

God the Father sent Jesus to help us. We can follow Jesus by being kind, honest, sharing, and forgiving.

🙏 What is prayer?

Prayer is talking AND listening to God.

  • 🌅 In the morning: make the sign of the cross + a prayer
  • 🌙 At night: say the Guardian Angel prayer + thank God
  • 🤝 Pray for others: the sick, the poor, friends and family
"Love God with all your heart" — this is the most important rule of all. — The Greatest Commandment

Going to Mass helps us learn more about God and show our love for Him. ⛪

28

Jesus Will Come Again — Heaven 🏠✨

▼

Heaven is our true and eternal home. When Jesus comes again, he will take us to live with Him forever.

❓ Why did God make me?

To know Him, to love Him, and to serve Him in this life — and to be happy with Him forever in Heaven!

⏳ Purgatory

A place of cleansing where the soul is purified before it goes to Heaven.

🛏️ St. Joseph

Many people pray to him for a happy death — because he died with Jesus and Mary at his side.

📿

Lent & Ash Wednesday

▼

Lent is a special 40-day season when we remember Jesus' Life, Death and Resurrection. It begins on Ash Wednesday and ends on Easter Sunday.

🌿 Where do the ashes come from?

The ashes are made by burning the palm leaves from the previous year's Palm Sunday! The priest puts them on our forehead in the sign of a cross.

The 3 Pillars of Lent

🙏

Prayer

Talk to God and listen to Him.

🍽️

Fasting

Give up something we love (like sweets) for God.

🤝

Almsgiving

Help the poor and share with others.

📖 Just like Jesus fasted and prayed in the desert for 40 days, we do the same during Lent! "Passion" means suffering — we remember Jesus' Passion.

Palm Sunday & the Triduum

▼

🌴 Palm Sunday

Jesus rode triumphantly into Jerusalem. People spread their cloaks on the road and waved palm leaves, shouting:

"Hosanna! Hosanna!"

Hosanna means "save us!"

The Triduum — The 3 Holiest Days 🕊️

"Triduum" means three days.

🍞

Holy Thursday

Last Supper · Jesus washes the disciples' feet

✝️

Good Friday

Jesus dies on the cross for us

⏳

Holy Saturday

Jesus rests in the tomb · we wait in silence

"Love one another as I have loved you." — Jesus' new commandment, Holy Thursday

📅 The Right Order

Palm Sunday → Holy Thursday → Good Friday → Easter Sunday → Ascension → Pentecost
