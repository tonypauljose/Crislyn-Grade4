📋 What's on the test

Straight from Crislyn's workbook. Tick these off as she gets confident with each one.

🔢 Chapter 1 — Numbers beyond 10,000

  • Number names → figures, and figures → number names (up to 7 digits)
  • Standard form & expanded form
  • Comparing numbers with <, >, =
  • Ascending & descending order
  • Predecessor & successor
  • Greatest & smallest number from given digits
  • Place value & face value
  • Roman numerals (both ways)

➕ Chapter 2 — Addition & Subtraction

  • Adding 5- and 6-digit numbers (with carrying)
  • Adding three numbers together
  • Subtracting with borrowing (including across zeros)
  • Properties of addition & subtraction
  • Checking subtraction by addition
  • Terms: sum, minuend, subtrahend, difference
  • Missing-number sentences
  • Word problems

🗓️ A simple 7-day revision plan

About 20 minutes a day. Repeat the cycle until the exam.

Day 1Quick Practice → Number names & figures, then Place & face value.
Day 2Quick Practice → Expanded/standard form and Compare & order.
Day 3Quick Practice → Predecessor/successor, Greatest & smallest, Roman numerals.
Day 4Speed Maths games + Quick Practice → Addition.
Day 5Quick Practice → Subtraction and Properties & terms.
Day 6Print the exam paper and do it at the table, then check with the answer key.
Day 7Sit the Full Timed Test (30 Q · 45 min) and review every answer.
